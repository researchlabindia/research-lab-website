Grievance Redressal

Investors can lodge complaints or grievances against the Research Analyst directly with the Research Analyst. The Research Analyst shall strive to redress the grievance immediately, but not later than 21 days from receipt of the grievance.

Mode of Filing a Complaint

  • SCORES 2.0

    Investors may lodge complaints through SCORES 2.0, a web-based centralized grievance redressal system of SEBI for facilitating effective grievance redressal in a time-bound manner.

    https://scores.sebi.gov.in

    Complaints or grievances against a Research Analyst are subject to a two-level review mechanism:

    • First review: Designated RAASB
    • Second review: SEBI
  • RAASB

    Investors may also lodge complaints through the designated email ID of the RAASB.

  • SMARTODR

    If an investor is not satisfied with the resolution provided by the Market Participant, the investor has the option to file the complaint or grievance on the SMARTODR platform for its resolution through online conciliation or arbitration.

  • Physical Complaints

    Investors may send physical complaints to:

    Office of Investor Assistance and Education,
    Securities and Exchange Board of India, SEBI Bhavan,
    Plot No. C4-A, ‘G’ Block, Bandra-Kurla Complex,
    Bandra (E), Mumbai - 400 051
